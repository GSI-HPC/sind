The video is AV1 with Opus audio in WebM, free codecs that browsers decode without extra packages; a browser that cannot decode AV1 (Safari without an AV1 hardware decoder) gets a note with a download link instead of a player that never starts. Until the manifest and the video both exist, as in a local build without renders, the shortcode renders nothing.
